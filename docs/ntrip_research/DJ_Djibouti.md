## Context Notes

- **Geodetic authority:** CERD (Centre d'Études et de Recherche de Djibouti) handles geodetic and geophysical matters. No GNSS CORS programme or NTRIP service has been identified on their website or in search results.
- **IGS station:** Djibouti hosts an IGS tracking station (DJIG, operated by Institut de Physique du Globe de Paris / IPGP) as part of the global geodetic tracking network; this is a scientific monitoring station, not a public RTK caster.
- No rtk2go or Centipede volunteer bases identified for Djibouti.
- No project announcement for a future RTK/CORS service found as of 2026-05-06.
- Djibouti's geodetic infrastructure development is at an early stage; the country is not part of any regional African CORS initiative that provides public NTRIP access (AFREF, TriGNet, etc.).
- Internet connectivity in Djibouti is provided primarily via submarine cable landing points; mobile broadband is growing but coverage remains limited outside Djibouti City.
