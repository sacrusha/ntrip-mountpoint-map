Free NTRIP corrections for better than ~50 cm GPS accuracy. Only need ~30 cm? Use Galileo HAS — free, global, no signup required. learn more

What this map shows. Public NTRIP mountpoints (RTK correction streams) that are free to access and capable of cm-level accuracy under good conditions, up to roughly 100 km from each station.

Colour bands around each station. Green < 10 km (excellent cm-RTK) · yellow-green 10–30 km (good) · amber 30–50 km (marginal, sometimes float) · pale red 50–100 km (float decimetre, multi-band receivers only).

Not here: sub-metre DGNSS services (use Galileo HAS instead), commercial / paid VRS networks (HxGN SmartNet, Trimble, etc.), and raw-observation networks like EPN that are for post-processing only.
