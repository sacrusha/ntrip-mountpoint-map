A just-in-time help system.

The two current guides try to answer every RTK question up front. This prototype answers the question the user is actually asking, where they are asking it. Three surfaces, no separate wiki page.

Try these

  • Click the L1+L2 chip in the popup ↓
  • Click the ? next to "Mount" ↓
  • Click "How to connect ↗" in the popup ↓
  • Click "Help" in the top bar ↑
  • In help, search "Emlid" →
  • In help, search "base station" →
  • First-run toast → "Take the tour" ↓

The principles

1 · Put help where the confusion is. "L1+L2," "Mount," "VRS" are technical terms dropped into a UI that a newcomer can't parse. Inline ? chips beside them expand to a two-sentence answer, not a new page.
2 · Answer the task, not the topic. Entries are phrased as user questions — "How do I connect this in Emlid Flow?" — not wiki topics. Each answer is 3–5 lines, with "Deep dive" progressive disclosure for the ~10% who need it.
3 · Don't block the map. The help drawer is a right-side slide-in. The map stays visible. Clicking a station behind it still updates context, so help + map work together.
4 · Replace the first-visit wall-of-text with a dismissible corner toast: tour or skip. Those who want the primer get a guided 4-step; those who don't are never blocked.

What disappears

Both #how-dialog (the 5-paragraph first-visit modal) and guide.html (the long-form wiki) are retired, along with their respective entry points (first-visit popup, "Guide" top-bar button, and the "Receiver setup guide →" link in the popup footer — now "How to connect ↗"). Their content is re-cut into ~13 task answers inside the drawer, searchable and cross-linked. The full-length prose isn't deleted — it lives behind "Deep dive" disclosures where relevant.
